# Djigo Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 25 July 2026
**Contact:** djigodestek@gmail.com

This policy explains what data the **Djigo** mobile application (the "App")
processes. By using the App, you accept this policy.

---

## 1. No account required

The App has **no** registration, login, email address or phone number. We do not
create an account that identifies you personally.

## 2. What data we process

### 2.1 Data that stays on your device

Your game settings, daily play credits and premium status are stored in your
device's local storage (`SharedPreferences`). This data is never sent to us. It
is removed when you uninstall the App.

### 2.2 Multi-phone mode (room game)

When you play in a shared room with friends, the following is stored temporarily
on **Google Firebase Realtime Database**:

- The room code
- The **nicknames players type themselves** (you do not need to enter a real name)
- In-game guesses/answers and scores

This data is required for the game to work. It is **deleted automatically** when
the room closes or when the player who created the room disconnects. The data is
hosted on Google servers located in the European Union (`europe-west1`).

We recommend not entering your real name, phone number or any other personal
information in the nickname field — it is visible to the other players in the room.

### 2.3 Advertising

To stay free, the App shows rewarded video ads through **Google AdMob**. While an
ad is served, Google may process:

- Your device advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID / iOS IDFA)
- IP address, device and operating system information
- Ad interactions (impression, view, click)

We neither collect nor store this data ourselves; it is processed directly by
Google. How Google uses data in apps:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

**Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland:** a
consent screen is shown on first launch. You may refuse personalised ads; in that
case ads are still shown but are not selected based on your interests. You can
change your choice later from the App's settings.

**iOS users:** your device's advertising identifier is not accessed unless you
grant permission in Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) dialog. If you
decline, the game works exactly the same, with all features intact.

### 2.4 Purchases

Premium subscriptions and one-time purchases are handled by **Google Play** or the
**Apple App Store**. We never see or store your card details. From the store we
receive only whether the user holds premium, and we keep that on the device.

### 2.5 Data we do not collect

- Location data
- Access to contacts, photos, camera or microphone
- Health, financial or biometric data
- Analytics or crash reporting data (the App contains no analytics SDK)

## 3. Who we share data with

We do not sell data. Only the following service providers are involved:

| Provider | Purpose | Policy |
|---|---|---|
| Google AdMob | Serving ads | https://policies.google.com/privacy |
| Google Firebase | Multi-phone mode infrastructure | https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy |
| Google Play Billing | Android purchases | https://policies.google.com/privacy |
| Apple App Store | iOS purchases | https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/ |

## 4. Legal basis (GDPR / UK GDPR)

- **Performance of a contract:** the room data required for the game to function.
- **Consent:** personalised advertising (consent screen in the EEA/UK, ATT
  permission on iOS).
- **Legitimate interests:** serving non-personalised advertising and preventing
  abuse.

## 5. Retention

Room data is deleted when the game ends or the connection drops. Data on your
device remains there until you uninstall the App. Retention of advertising data
is governed by Google's policies.

## 6. Your rights

Because we keep no accounts, we hold no persistent record linked to you. Even so,
if you wish to exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure or objection
to processing, write to **djigodestek@gmail.com**. We respond within 30 days at
the latest.

To delete all data on your device, simply uninstall the App. To reset your
advertising identifier: Android → Settings > Google > Ads; iOS → Settings >
Privacy & Security > Tracking.

If you are in the EEA or the UK, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with
your local data protection authority.

## 7. Children's privacy

The App is **intended for adults** and is not designed for users under 18. We do
not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has
entered data, write to us and we will delete it.

## 8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date on this page. For
material changes we will notify you inside the App.
